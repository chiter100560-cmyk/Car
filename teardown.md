# Competitor bad-review teardown

Every 1-, 2- and 3-star review that exists for the five named competitors, pulled
August 29 2026, plus three supplementary operators added because three of the five
turned out to have almost nothing negative written about them.

Method: Google's own review panel, sorted **Lowest rating**, scrolled to the end.
Yelp, filtered by star rating, including the "not currently recommended" archive.
Every quote below is verbatim. Nothing here is summarised or invented.

---

| Family Mobile Window Tint | Google | 4.9 / 135 | 2 | 0 | 0 |

GoFilms has 351 reviews and its lowest-rated review in the whole profile is a single
4-star. Platinum's only 1-star has no text in it. That is not an accident — high-volume
mobile operators in this market run hard review-solicitation, and the negatives that do
land get buried. **Google reviews are not where you will find these competitors' weak
spots.** You find them on Yelp, and on operators who stopped chasing reviews.

So the ranking below is built from 11 reviews across the five named competitors plus
**37 more** from three high-volume operators running the same business model:

- **JP's Mobile Car Wash**, Miami — Yelp 4.5 / 197, with 14 one-star, 2 two-star, 8 three-star
- **Superior Tint of Boca**, Boca Raton — Yelp 4.8 / 368, with 13 one-star
- **B & B Mobile Detailing**, Broward — Google 5.0 / 414, 1 one-star

JP's is in Miami and Superior Tint is a shop, not mobile. They are outside your
territory. They are in here because the failure modes transfer exactly, and because
five clean profiles give you nothing to build rules from.
