# Automotive window film sourcing for a mobile tint startup — Coral Springs, FL
All prices observed **2026-09-26** unless noted. Per-car math uses Flexfilm's own yield guide (40"x100' ≈ 8 vehicles ≈ 12.5 ft/car); multiply $/car by 1.23 if you run SUV-heavy at ~6.5 cars/roll.

## Bottom line on the owner's beliefs
- **3M / XPEL / LLumar-FormulaOne / SunTek / Solar Gard / Hüper Optik / KAVACA are dealer-gated — mostly TRUE**, with two holes: (a) genuine **Solar Gard "Smoke"** and **Avery Dennison** rolls are sold to anyone by Metro Restyling; (b) **Global** is NOT gated — buyglobalfilm.com (Houston) and Express Window Films (Orlando service center) show prices publicly.
- **Flexfilm, Tinters Supply, Tint Depot sell to anyone — TRUE.** Flexfilm and Tint Depot allow guest checkout; Tinters Supply (Miami Lakes, ~25 mi from Coral Springs) requires an account but the registration form asks for nothing beyond normal billing fields.
- **Carbon $27–37/car — roughly TRUE for lifetime-warranty carbon** ($205–$279/roll → $26–35 at 8 cars/roll; $31–43 at 6.5). Cheaper 5-year-warranty carbon exists ($170–$175/roll → $21–22/car).
- **Ceramic $44–58/car — TRUE only for entry ceramic** ($310–$439/roll → $39–55 at 8 cars/roll). High-IR ceramic (92–98% IR) is $449–$824/roll → **$56–103/car**. The owner's ceramic number is optimistic for anything marketed as "IR ceramic".

## 4. Florida compliance arithmetic (2026 statutes, verified on leg.state.fl.us)
- §316.2953 fronts: ≥28% transmittance, ≤25% reflectance, "when applied to and tested on the glass". §316.2954: behind driver ≥15% (≤35% refl.); **multipurpose passenger vehicle ≥6%**; rear window may be darker only with both side mirrors. §316.2951: MPV = ≤10 persons, "constructed either on a truck chassis or with special features for occasional off-road operation" (pickups, body-on-frame SUVs clearly; unibody crossovers are a gray area). **§316.2955(2): "Every percentage measurement… is subject to a tolerance of plus or minus 3 percent."** §316.2955(3): department-approved meters presumed accurate. **§316.2955(1): installers must affix a compliance label with trade name and business info on the inside left door jamb — you must stock these.** Non-moving infraction; $116 first / $211 repeat per window per a third-party guide (not in statute). https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0300-0399/0316/Sections/0316.2955.html
- Factory glass: "70% to 80%" (NuVision), "70–75%" (SunGuard), "75–80%" (TintLaws/others). Reading = glass × film. To read ≥28% without leaning on tolerance you need film ≥37% on 75% glass (≥35% on 80% glass); with the ±3% tolerance the floor is 25% → film ≥33%. **Nominal "35%" ≠ measured**: Terraflex 35 = 36% (reads 27–29%), Duraflex 35 = 34.7% (26–28%), Nanoflex 35 = 35%, Tint Depot Ceramic IR 35 = 36%, **Panaflex 35 = 31.9% (reads 24–25.5% — fails)**, Tint Depot Carbon 35 = 31% (fails). SunGuard's advice to install 30% "lands right around 28%" contradicts the multiplication and I would not follow it.
- Rears: sedan floor 15% (12% with tolerance): 20% film → 15–17.5% OK; 15% film → 11–12% fails. MPV floor 6% (3% with tolerance): 5% film on clear glass → 4–6% (Duraflex 5 measures 8% → 6–6.4% OK; Terraflex 5 = 5% → tolerance only). On factory privacy glass (commonly cited ~15–25% VLT; meter it) any 5–20% film stacks to 1–5% → illegal; 35–50% film → 5–12%.
- Reflectance is a non-issue for these films (VLR 5–10% vs. 25% cap).
- **Recommended starter inventory**: 2× 40"x100' at nominal 35–40% (use one whose measured VLT ≥36%: Terraflex 35/40, Duraflex 35, Nanoflex 35); 1× 40"x100' 20% (sedan rears); 1× 40"x100' 5% (SUV/truck rears — legal only on clear MPV glass and only with tolerance; most-requested look); 1× 20"x100' 35% for fronts-only "match the privacy glass" jobs. Add a 70% ceramic 20"x100' later for windshields.
- **Tint meter**: Tint-Chek TC3800 two-piece, 0.1% resolution, NIST standards, **$229** at tint-chek.com (5+ $219) — Flexfilm resells it for $326.20, don't. Cheaper: Laser Labs Inspector II TM2000 **$179** (laser-labs.com), Enforcer II $119. I could not find an FLHSMV list of approved devices.

## 5. Film types
Generic ranges @35% VLT (TintLaws): dyed TSER 25–35%/IR 15–25%, fades purple in 3–5 yrs; carbon TSER 35–50%/IR 40–60%; ceramic TSER 45–58%/IR 70–85%; nano-ceramic TSER 55–70%/IR 90–97%. Verified sheets: Duraflex 35 TSER 42%; Terraflex 35 TSER 50%; Avery Encore 35 TSER 40%; Tint Depot Ceramic IR 35 TSER 50%; Panaflex 35 TSER 61%; Nanoflex 35 TSER 60%; Avery Encore Supreme IR 35 TSER 57%; Solar Gard Solstice 35 TSER 40%. Compare on **TSER**, not "IR rejection" — vendors quote the flattering 900–1000 nm band (Terraflex 5%: 74% vs 67% wideband).
- **$100 launch offer: lifetime-warranty carbon** (Duraflex/Terraflex/Cheetah Carbon Pro/Tinters Supply HX/Scorpion) at $25–35/car. Skip dyed (purples) and 5-year films (Tint Depot Carbon, Tint Wholesale Nano-Carbon Standard, Ceramic Stealth) for anything you warranty. **Later**: entry IR ceramic (Tint Club, Tint Wholesale IR Nano, Tinters Supply Ceramic, Tint Depot Ceramic IR, Panaflex 40) at $40–60/car.
- Brands that bubble/fail: **I could not verify brand-specific failure reports** — TintDude, Reddit, Amazon reviews and Facebook were all blocked. What I have: dyed economy films purple by mechanism; sun-tint.com only says poor film "can bubble, peel… turn purple"; MotoShield Trustpilot has one 1-star (DIY difficulty, not failure); a 5thGenRams poster reported shops replacing 1–2-yr-old 3M (anecdote) and another knocked Panaflex's heat test vs. Nanoflex. Mitigation: buy 40"x10' sample rolls (Tint Club $19.99–39.99; Flexfilm sample rolls exist) and test shrink/adhesive before committing.
