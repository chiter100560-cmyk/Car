## 1. Current verification flow (2025–2026) for a new service-area business (SAB)

**Is video the default?** Google does not name a "default." Its verification page lists five methods (phone/SMS, email, live video call, postcard, video recording) and says: "The verification options available to you depend on your business type, public info, region, or business hours" and "Verification methods are automatically determined by Google and can't be changed." Live video call "may not be available for all businesses"; postcard "isn't available for all businesses." If you can't verify with what's offered: "contact support." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242?hl=en
Practitioner view (JXT Group, Apr 7 2026): postcard "appears less frequently for service-area businesses compared to previous years, with video becoming the predominant method." Same article: Google added a setup prompt "What best describes your business?" (physical location / mobile service / service-area / unstaffed facility / online-only); choosing "online-only" wrongly can trigger compliance problems; making profile edits during verification and a failed video submission can remove other verification options. — https://www.jxtgroup.com/google-business-profile-verification-in-2026-new-warnings-video-requirements-how-to-stay-compliant/

**What the video must show (Google, verbatim)** — https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705?hl=en
- Location: "Include street signs, building numbers, nearby businesses, or familiar places around your business." SAB variant: "record street signs, nearby landmarks, or other identifiers at your business address."
- Business existence: "Include your storefront, showroom, or business signage. Make sure your business name is clearly printed on a permanent fixture." SAB variant: "Show the professional tools, equipment, or products you use for work."
- Proof of management: "Record things only an employee can access, like opening a cash register, kitchen, storage room, or using a point-of-sale system." SAB variant: "Record yourself performing a service or accessing business-only assets" or show "business documents like a business permit, invoice, or utility bill."
- Do NOT include: "Sensitive info like bank account, tax, or ID numbers. Private info about you or others. Other people's faces."

**Recording rules (Google, verbatim, same page):** "An unedited, unique, and complete recording with no breaks"; "At least 30 seconds long"; "Recorded and uploaded from a mobile device through your Business Profile"; "You must capture the footage live as pre-recorded videos aren't allowed." Google publishes no maximum length. Practitioners: ~2 minutes, one continuous shot (Search Engine Journal, Sherry Bonelli, Sep 6 2024 — https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-business-profile-video-verification/462489/); 1–2 minutes, consider recording at 720p instead of 1080p to avoid upload failures (Local Splash, Nov 12 2024 — https://localsplash.com/google-business-profile-video-verification/); keep under 2 minutes / use a lower-quality setting if uploads fail (Local Search Forum thread — https://localsearchforum.com/threads/why-is-google-video-verification-failing-and-what-should-i-do-next.60421/).

**Turnaround:** Google: "This process can take up to 5 business days." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705?hl=en. Real-world (Two Four Media, Feb 17 2026): "3–5 days (fast approvals)", "7–10 days (common delays)", "14+ days if additional review is triggered." — https://www.twofourmedia.com/post/google-business-profile-verification-problems-in-2026-what-to-do-when-video-verification-fails

**On failure:** Google: "Your video didn't meet all the requirements. You can record a new one" (Try again). — https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705?hl=en. Google publishes NO lockout period; none of the sources found gives a fixed one. "No more ways to verify" means "Google is not currently offering another verification method for that Business Profile" (GBP Suspensions, Sep 17 2026 — https://gbpsuspensions.com/no-more-ways-to-verify-google-business/). Forum guidance: "keep trying until it works, or it gives you an error" (Elizabeth Rule, Jul 10 2023, LSF thread above). Two Four Media: after a second failed cycle, open a support ticket / request manual review with business license, website link, signage photos, explanation of the location setup.

**Appeal path (Google, verbatim):** Appeals tool at business.google.com ("/business/?p=manage_appeals"): sign in, pick the profile, review the reason, "Submit Appeal." "Appeal reviews and decisions can take up to 5 business days." Statuses: Submitted, Approved, Not Approved, Can't be appealed, Eligible for appeal. "Do not submit multiple appeals for the same issue before receiving a decision"; do not create a new profile for the same business while an appeal is under review. Evidence: "you must submit your supporting evidence within 60 minutes or it won't be attached to your appeal." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/13597551?hl=en. Change reported Jul 8 2026: evidence now attaches inside the appeal form via one multi-file button, replacing the separate 60-minute form (Digital Applied — https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/google-business-profile-appeal-evidence-uploads-2026). Whitespark (Miriam Ellis, Apr 27 2026): zip file max 20 MB; decisions "might take as much as six weeks. There is no way to expedite this." — https://whitespark.ca/guides/ultimate-guide-to-recovering-from-gbp-suspension/

**Documents Google accepts (verbatim list):** "Official business registration," "A business license," "Tax certificates," and utility bills ("Electricity," "Phone," "Water," "Internet"). "Check that the business name and address match the profile you want to make an appeal for." If denied you can request "an additional review to a denied request." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/4569145?hl=en. Note: utility bills were briefly dropped from the list in mid-2025, then restored (Search Engine Roundtable, Jul 10 2025 — https://www.seroundtable.com/google-business-profiles-droos-utility-bill-39742.html). Insurance COIs and supplier invoices are NOT on Google's list; practitioners still submit them as supporting evidence (GBP Suspensions link above). No document-only verification is offered up front; documents are an appeal/support mechanism, not a menu option.

**Florida/Broward documents for a sole-prop DBA (to have ready before recording):**
- Sunbiz fictitious name registration: $50; online filers get a confirmation letter (name, registration number, date) "within 24 hours of your registration's posting"; the name "must be advertised at least once in a newspaper" in the county (self-certified, no proof required); filing is public record. — https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/start-business/efile/fl-fictitious-name-registration/
- Coral Springs Business Tax Receipt: required for home-based businesses; "a copy of the registration from the State of Florida must be provided" for a fictitious name; processed "within 7-10 business days"; expires Sept 30 yearly; you cannot operate before it is approved. — https://www.coralsprings.gov/Government/Departments/Business-Tax-Office/Business-Tax-Registration
- Broward County Business Tax Receipt is separate and also required ("one Broward business can need both a city receipt and a county receipt"), $27–$150, via BTExpress; needs Sunbiz/fictitious-name record and city receipt/zoning approval. (Third-party guide; county PDF 404'd.) — https://businesslicenseguide.com/broward-county-fl-business-tax-receipt-guide/
- Blunt point: as a sole proprietor the home utility bill is in Ziyad's name, not "Car Mommy." Google wants name + address to match the profile. Get the BTRs issued as "Car Mommy" (or "Ziyad [Last] DBA Car Mommy") at 6160 Wiles Rd [Unit], and have at least one supplier invoice/insurance COI in the Car Mommy name at that address.

## 2. SAB-specific rules

- **Hide the address.** Google: "If you're a service-area business, you should hide your business address from customers. For example, if you're a plumber and run your business from your residential address, clear the address from your Business Profile." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en. Also: "If you don't serve customers at your business address, remove your address from your Business Profile." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en
- **You still enter the real address.** Google: "Create your Business Profile for your actual, real-world location" (guidelines link above), and the video instructions for SABs refer to identifiers "at your business address" (video page above). Local Falcon (Nov 7 2025): you register the home address so Google can verify you, then hide it and set service areas. — https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-google-business-profile-video-verification. Whitespark (Apr 27 2026): "home addresses are acceptable if hidden" for SABs.
- **Virtual office rule:** "Service-area businesses can't list a 'virtual' office unless that office is staffed during business hours." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en
- **Service-area limits.** "You can have up to 20 service areas." "The boundaries of your overall area shouldn't be more than about 2 hours of driving time from where your business is based." Areas are cities/postal codes/other designations; no radius option. — https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en. Same 2-hour language in the guidelines page. The 20-cap dates to Nov 2019 (Local Search Forum — https://localsearchforum.com/threads/maximum-of-20-service-areas-on-a-gmb-listing.55228/). Your 7 areas are well inside both limits. "West Boca" is not a municipality: add it by ZIP code(s) rather than "Boca Raton" if you don't want to imply east Boca.
- **Ranking distance is measured from the hidden address.** Whitespark (Darren Shaw, Oct 28 2024): removing service areas "had absolutely no impact on his rankings whatsoever"; with a hidden address "the only address it can use is the address that the listing was originally verified at." — https://whitespark.ca/blog/2023-do-service-areas-impact-rankings-for-local-seo/. Expect to rank best in Coral Springs/Parkland/Coconut Creek/Margate and weaker in Deerfield/west Boca.
- **Must the owner be the one in the video?** Google says "Record yourself performing a service or accessing business-only assets" and the video must be recorded "through your Business Profile," i.e., by the signed-in account holder. Owners and managers ("authorized representatives") may verify (Local Search Forum, Stefan Somborac, Aug 5 2026 — https://localsearchforum.com/threads/do-you-have-to-be-a-business-profile-owner-to-verify-a-profile-can-managers-complete-video-verification.63334/); SEJ: the person should be "either the owner or manager." So Ziyad records while signed into the profile's Google account. Alex can appear doing work, but Google says don't include "Other people's faces" — film his hands/the job, not his face.
- **Another business already at the address (the pool company).** Google: distinct businesses at one address "can each have their own Business Profiles if they're distinct businesses with different names and clearly visible differences in signage"; "Multiple profiles have the same address" is a duplicate trigger only when they represent the same business. — https://support.google.com/business/answer/12756178?hl=en. Guidelines: co-located businesses "should each have their own profile." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en. Whitespark's eligibility test for a second listing at one address: unique business name, unique entrance with signage, unique phone number, unique primary category. — https://whitespark.ca/guides/ultimate-guide-to-recovering-from-gbp-suspension/. RankLocalToday (Jul 21 2026): "each business needs its own distinguishable suite, unit, or floor number"; use the real unit number "not invented identifiers." — https://ranklocaltoday.com/blog/multiple-google-business-profiles-same-address. Growth for Local (Jun 22 2026): separate legal setup (DBA), own EIN, own license, own phone, different category, own sign; "most second listings at the same address need a video verification"; suspension triggers include near-identical names, shared phone, identical categories, no visible signage, fabricated suite numbers. — https://growthforlocal.com/blog/two-google-business-profile-at-the-same-address/
  Practical read for Car Mommy: the pool company is unrelated, different name/category/phone, so it is not a policy problem; it raises the odds of video verification and of a human reviewer looking harder. Enter your correct unit number, never share the pool company's phone/website, keep their signage/vehicles out of frame, and put "Car Mommy" signage on your own door/storage.

## 3. Naming rules

- Google: "Your name should reflect your business's real-world name, as used consistently on your storefront, website, stationery, and as known to customers." Not allowed in the name: marketing taglines, service/product info (example "Verizon Wireless 4G LTE"), location details, containment info, hours, phone/URLs. — https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en
- "Car Mommy" alone is fine. "Car Mommy Window Tint & Detailing" is a descriptor violation unless that exact string is the registered DBA and is what customers see everywhere. Whitespark (Miriam Ellis, Oct 28 2024): the DBA route is the legitimate way to add descriptors, but you must then use it on signage/vehicles, website logo and text, all citations and social, and update GBP last; "Including unnecessary information in your business name isn't permitted, and could result in the suspension of your Business Profile." — https://whitespark.ca/blog/everything-that-can-go-right-and-wrong-with-your-local-business-name/. Whitespark's suspension guide (Apr 2026) says the same: fix it via DBA, not by editing the GBP title. Reported 2025 study (Birdeye citing Reinstate Labs, search snippet only): name violations ≈34% of suspensions — https://birdeye.com/blog/google-business-profile-guidelines/
- Decision: pick the name BEFORE verifying and match it exactly on Sunbiz, BTRs, website header, magnets, cards, invoices. Do not rename after verification: business name is a "sensitive" field; Whitespark advises spacing sensitive edits (name, address, pin, phone, category, URL) to one per week, and JXT reports post-verification changes can trigger re-verification. Blunt recommendation: launch as "Car Mommy" and put "window tint & detailing" in the description, services, and website title — not the name.

## 4. Categories and setup

**Exact category names that exist (checked against a full category list updated May 9 2026 — https://daltonluka.com/blog/google-my-business-categories):** "Auto window tinting service", "Window tinting service", "Car detailing service", "Car wash", "Self service car wash", "RV detailing service", "Boat detailing service", "Vehicle wrapping service", "Auto glass repair service". NOT in the list: "Auto detailing service", "Mobile car wash", "Mobile detailing", "Ceramic coating service", "Paint protection film service".
- Google confirmed in 2021 that SAB (hidden-address) listings are blocked from "Window tinting service"; "Google has suggested using the category 'Auto window tinting service'." (LocalU, Joy Hawkins, Aug 6 2021 — https://localu.org/google-my-business-disables-select-categories-for-service-area-businesses/). Could not confirm the block is still active in 2026; expect it.
- Google's rule: "as few categories as possible" and "as specific as possible." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en
- Recommendation: Primary "Auto window tinting service" (if tint is the lead offer; otherwise "Car detailing service"); secondary "Car detailing service" (or the reverse). Skip "Car wash" (implies a fixed facility; adds nothing for a mobile detailer) and "Vehicle wrapping service" unless you actually wrap. GMB Everywhere's detailing template uses primary "Car detailing service" with "Car wash", "Auto window tinting service", "Vehicle wrapping service" as secondaries — https://www.gmbeverywhere.com/categories/car-detailing-service-google-business-profile

**Services with prices:** Google: go to the profile, "Select Edit services," pick suggested services or "create and add custom services"; "add info such as price and description"; "Custom services that violate policy will be automatically rejected." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/9455399?hl=en. Price field options are "Fixed price" or "From $..."; descriptions up to 300 characters (GMB Everywhere, Feb 20 2026 — https://www.gmbeverywhere.com/guides/add-optimize-services-google-business-profile.html). Put "Front windows tint — from $X", "Full car tint — from $X", "Interior detail — from $X", etc.

**Hours:** Google: "Provide your regular customer-facing hours of operation." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en. Publishing Saturday/Sunday hours is fine as long as you actually answer/book on those days; don't mark 24 hours.

**Review link / QR:** Google: Business Profile → "Read reviews" → "Get more reviews"; copy the link or download the QR; "Currently, reviews QR codes can only be generated on a computer browser, not on mobile devices." Suggested placements: receipts, thank-you emails, end of chats. Same page: "Offering incentives, like free or discounted goods or services, to customers in exchange for reviews is considered fake engagement and is strictly prohibited." — https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815?hl=en. Community guide confirms desktop-only and the 4 steps — https://support.google.com/business/community-guide/389993756/how-to-get-a-business-profile-review-short-link-or-qr-code?hl=en. The generated short link is of the g.page/r/… form (third-party, not opened — https://wiremo.co/blog/how-to-get-your-google-review-link/).

**Review policy (Google Maps UGC policy, verbatim):** merchants may not "offer incentives – such as payment, discounts, free goods and/or services - in exchange for posting any review or revision or removal of a negative review"; "should not require or pressure users to leave ratings or write reviews while on the premises, nor should they request that specific content be included"; may not "discourage or prohibit negative reviews, or selectively solicit positive reviews from customers"; reviews from "current or former employment, a contractual or consultory relationship, or other professional or personal affiliations" are conflicts of interest; staff may not be told to "solicit a certain number of reviews" or reviews "that include specific content." Permitted: "solicit or encourage the posting of content that does represent a genuine experience, without offering incentives." — https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en. Timeline: on-premises language added Feb 20 2026; staff-quota / staff-name-mention bans added Apr 17 2026 (ppc.land, citing Product Expert Amy Toman — https://ppc.land/google-tightens-maps-review-policy-staff-names-and-quotas-now-banned/ ; Digital Shift Media — https://digitalshiftmedia.com/google-review-policy-changes-2026-home-services/).

**Your $100 "practice-price" tints:** A low price offered to every customer, with no mention of reviews, is pricing, not an incentive; the prohibition is on anything given "in exchange for" a review (policy above; Applause: "Leave a review and get $X off" is explicitly forbidden — https://www.applausehq.com/blog/googles-rules-for-incentivizing-reviews ; WiserReview, updated Jul 14 2026 — https://wiserreview.com/blog/google-review-incentives/). Rules for doing it safely: same price for everyone; never say "in exchange for a review"; ask AFTER you leave the driveway (send the link by text/email), don't hand them a phone; don't ask them to name Alex; never ask friends, family, or Alex to review (conflict of interest); don't only ask happy customers (gating). Expect Google to filter reviews from brand-new or inactive Google accounts.

**Velocity/filtering risk (Whitespark, Miriam Ellis, May 14 2026):** review-loss triggers include "Increased review velocity", "Inconsistent review velocity" (bursts), "Use of QR codes – … taking customers straight from your physical location to your … review form", review kiosks/shared IP, and "Inactive or remote reviewers – … a profile that has left few or no reviews in the past"; a penalty "30-day review block" exists. — https://whitespark.ca/blog/missing-google-reviews-checklists-for-troubleshooting-review-loss/. Translation: 10 reviews in the first week from new accounts looks fake; spread the $100 jobs over weeks and ask each customer once.

## 5. Expert tips for passing as a home-based SAB, and common 2025–2026 failure reasons

- Home-based SAB shot advice (Local Search Forum thread; JeffClevelandTN Jun 22 2023, Brian-TGL Nov 16 2023, Justin Mosebach Jan 31 2024): show the street number on the home or mailbox, do a quick 360° spin, branded vehicle in the driveway, unlock the front/office door, computer logged into accounting/booking/website admin, business licenses, business cards close-up, equipment and marketing material; "equipment alone is insufficient" — the video must connect the verifier to the business; the video "isn't published on the live GBP." — https://localsearchforum.com/threads/why-does-google-require-a-video-of-a-service-only-business-located-in-a-personal-residence-for-verification.60343/
- SEJ (Sep 6 2024) failure list: "Plain white branded vehicles without company identification", "Generic software applications instead of industry-specific tools", "Unmarked roads or vacant lots instead of clear address markers", "Temporary signage (vinyl banners) rather than permanent business identification." — https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-business-profile-video-verification/462489/
- Boomcycle (Oct 21 2025): "The vehicle must clearly display the business name through a sticker or magnetic sign. Film the vehicle in a location that clearly shows the service area, with street signs or landmarks"; failures: inconsistent NAP across web/citations, wrong address types (PO box, unstaffed virtual office), inadequate signage, personal info visible. — https://boomcycle.com/blog/google-business-profile-verification-expert-tips-2026/
- Local Splash (Nov 12 2024): show license plates on vehicles, record yourself accessing the vehicle, keep faces/sensitive docs out, 720p, 1–2 min. — https://localsplash.com/google-business-profile-video-verification/
- Local Falcon (Nov 7 2025): failures = poor video quality, mismatching business information, missing required elements; owner appearance not required. — https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-google-business-profile-video-verification
- GBP Suspensions (Sep 17 2026): before retrying, search for duplicate/existing profiles, audit the profile against real-world documents, plan a stronger video. — https://gbpsuspensions.com/no-more-ways-to-verify-google-business/
- Sterling Sky (search snippet only; page blocked): "The number one reason for video verification failure is that multiple locations share the same service territory" — applies to owners creating multiple profiles; not your case, but do not create a second Car Mommy profile for any reason. — https://www.sterlingsky.ca/the-google-business-profile-2-hour-rule-is-a-myth-why-your-google-verification-is-actually-failing/
- Your specific risk factors: (1) no permanent signage and a multi-unit address → the house/unit number and street signs must be unmistakable; (2) owner isn't the installer → prove management via keys, storage, documents, software, not a service demo; (3) another business at the address → your own unit number, own phone, own signage, nothing of theirs in frame; (4) no LLC → the DBA + BTRs are your "official business registration"; get them before you record.

## Do this in order

1. Lock the name: "Car Mommy." Register the Sunbiz fictitious name ($50, run the newspaper notice), get an EIN (needed anyway for a separate business identity per the two-businesses guides), then the Coral Springs BTR (home-based; attach the Sunbiz confirmation; 7–10 business days) and the Broward County BTR. Get a business phone number that is not the pool company's or a personal line already on another profile. Insurance COI in the business name.
2. Build the real-world footprint with the identical name/phone/URL: carmommy.us header + footer with phone and "serving Coral Springs, Parkland…" (no street address), a contact page, domain email; vehicle magnets/decals (name + phone + carmommy.us) and a rear-window decal; polo/T-shirts with logo; business cards; printed invoice/receipt template; a rigid "Car Mommy" sign for the storage door/rack (not a vinyl banner); labeled tool bag/bins.
3. Pre-flight: search Google Maps for "Car Mommy" and for the address; make sure no old/unclaimed profile exists and the pool company's listing doesn't use your phone or website. Check Facebook/Instagram/Yelp names match exactly.
4. Create the profile from the Google account you will keep long-term (Workspace account on carmommy.us is ideal). Name "Car Mommy"; business type = serves customers at their locations / mobile / service-area (never online-only); enter the full address WITH the unit number for verification, then choose not to show it; service areas = Coral Springs, Parkland, Coconut Creek, Margate, Tamarac, Deerfield Beach, plus west Boca by ZIP; primary "Auto window tinting service", secondary "Car detailing service"; phone; website; hours including Sat/Sun; short description mentioning mobile tint and detailing.
5. Stage the props (below), pick a bright dry day, phone at 720p, storage/vehicle keys in hand, documents on a table inside, laptop open on the booking system/website admin. Rehearse the walk twice; target 60–120 s.
6. Choose video verification in the profile prompt, record in one take, upload on the spot. Then touch nothing on the profile for up to 5 business days (edits during review are a known re-verification trigger).
7. If rejected: read the stated reason ("no nearby area shown", "missing proof of authorization", etc.), fix exactly that, re-record. If it says "No more ways to verify": open the appeals tool; if status is "Eligible for appeal," submit once with DBA confirmation, both BTRs, tax/EIN letter, a Car Mommy-named utility/insurance/supplier document — all showing 6160 Wiles Rd [Unit]; otherwise contact support with the dated attempt log. Do not create a new profile.
8. After "Verified": add services with "From $" prices, 4–6 photos (branded vehicle, equipment, cards, a job in progress), Q&A, generate the review link + QR on a desktop, set up review replies. Send the review link by text after each job; pace requests; never tie them to the $100 price. Space any sensitive edits one per week and never add descriptors to the name.

## Shot list (one continuous take, ~90 s, Ziyad recording while signed into the profile's Google account)
