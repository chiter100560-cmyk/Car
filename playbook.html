02

What changed, and what was wrong

Coral Springs is a better base than Boca — but it moves you to Broward

Paperwork changed

Wiles Rd sits in the middle of exactly the customer you want. Parkland, Coconut Creek and Margate are 5–10 minutes out; Tamarac, west Boca and Deerfield are inside 20. All single-family, all driveways, hoses and outdoor outlets. From here your 20-minute rule covers more driveway homes than it would have from Boca.

The cost: Coral Springs is Broward County. Palm Beach County and the City of Boca Raton are off your list entirely — it's now Broward County + City of Coral Springs. Sales tax is unchanged at 7% (6% state + 1% county surtax). Section 03 C below is already corrected.

Two problems with 6160 Wiles Rd — check both before you touch Google

Verify first

It has unit numbers on it. That means a multi-unit building, not a house. Fine for verification if you actually live there and can film with your equipment on site. Not fine if it's an office suite or a mailbox — a video walkthrough will fail, and failed attempts are what trigger the lockout.

Another service business is already listed at that address — a pool cleaning company. Sharing an address isn't fatal, but Google applies extra scrutiny when a second service-area business appears at one it already knows. Expect a harder verification, and have your LLC certificate, your lease or utility bill, and your equipment all in the shot.

Also: no garage and no driveway at a condo means nowhere to store a pressure washer, nowhere to keep film flat and clean, and nowhere to park a marked van later. Your rinseless-first plan already handles the first two. The van question you ask the city about.

Your "nobody does both" edge is weaker on this turf than it was in Delray

Re-check the thesis

Mobile Detailers Inc, on N University Dr in Coral Springs, already advertises detailing, ceramic coating, paint correction and window tinting. That is your exact concept, on your exact street, already running. Platinum Auto Detailing sits between Coral Springs and Parkland with 70+ five-star reviews and both mobile and drop-off. And One Call Detailing — the 571-review Pompano operation — runs a dedicated Coral Springs landing page with a $20-off code, so they are actively buying your neighborhood.

This doesn't kill the plan. It kills the lazy version of it. "We do both" is no longer enough on its own here — the remaining edges are Saturday and Sunday availability, genuinely fast response, and being the one that shows up on time. Which is exactly why the bad-review teardown matters more now, not less.

The tint sticker is required — and the statute number was wrong

Criminal, not a fine

Last time you were pointed at 316.2953. The labeling rule is Fla. Stat. 316.2955; penalties are in 316.2956. Half the tint blogs online say Florida needs no compliance sticker. They are wrong, and they are the reason installers get burned.

The installer must affix a pressure-sensitive, self-destructive, non-removable vinyl label to the inside left door jamb, showing the film's trade name and your business name. Selling or installing film in violation is a second-degree misdemeanor — that lands on you, not the customer. Order labels the same day you order your first roll of film.

Nobody told you about sales tax. This is the one that eats people.

Register before car #1

Florida doesn't tax labor by itself. But you're not selling labor by itself — you apply chemicals, wax, coating, film. The moment materials transfer to the customer's car, the entire bill becomes taxable, not just the product. Tint is unambiguous: the film is tangible property you're installing.

Palm Beach and Broward are both at 7%. On a $250 detail that's $16.35. Ten cars a week is $8,500 a year you would owe out of your own pocket, plus penalty and interest, if you never collected it. Register on Form DR-1 — it's free — and price all-in so the customer never sees a surprise line.

Local Services Ads: eligible now, but the badge isn't

Deprioritize

Google expanded its auto categories, so car wash and detailing can run Local Services Ads. But the Google Verified badge is currently unavailable for the auto, beauty and dining verticals — and the badge is the entire reason LSA converts. Without it you're paying per lead for a plain listing.

Revised ad order: 1. Google Business Profile + reviews (free, biggest lever by far) → 2. Google Search ads on buying-intent keywords → 3. Instagram/Meta before-and-afters → 4. LSA once the badge returns. Yelp is last; in this corridor it's where the tint shops live, not the detailers.

WindshieldNon-reflective only, above the AS-1 line
ReflectivityMax 25% front and back sides
Banned colorsRed, amber, blue — any window

VLT means visible light transmission — how much light gets through. Lower number, darker film. A customer asking for "limo tint" on their front windows is asking you to commit a misdemeanor. Quote them the legal number and move on; the ticket goes to them and the review goes to you.

The physical problem nobody mentions

Tint hates the outdoors. Airborne dust, sun affecting adhesive cure time, afternoon rain. Cheap dyed film tolerates a driveway; ceramic really wants a controlled space. Ask "do you have a garage we can pull into?" before you book a tint job — not when you arrive.
