# Filing the LLC on Sunbiz — field by field

Everything verified against the Florida Department of State on 2026-08-29. Fees
and the annual-report penalty are quoted from dos.fl.gov directly, not from a blog.

**Do it yourself. Do not pay a filing service $300 for this.** It is a web form
and takes about 20 minutes once you have the four answers in the next section.

---

## What it costs

| Item | Fee | Get it? |
|---|---|---|
| Articles of Organization | $100.00 | Required |
| Registered Agent Designation | $25.00 | Required |
| Certified Copy | $30.00 | Skip |
| Certificate of Status | $5.00 | Skip |
| **What you actually pay** | **$125.00** | |

All four together is $160. Skip the last two — the free downloadable image of your
filed Articles plus the EIN letter is what a bank actually asks for. If a specific
bank insists on a Certificate of Status you can order one later for $5.

**Then diary this immediately:** the annual report is **$138.75**, due between
January 1 and **May 1** every year. Miss May 1 and Florida adds a **$400 late fee**.
It is not prorated and it is not negotiable. Set a phone reminder for **March 1,
2027** — not May 1, so there is slack.

## Four things to decide before you open the form

Every one of these goes on a public record you cannot casually change later.

### 1. The name

Must end in `LLC`, `L.L.C.` or `Limited Liability Company`. Your four rules from
the playbook still hold: no city in it, no "Detailing" in it, spellable over the
phone at 60mph, .com free.

Already gone: drivewayautocare, doorstepautocare, curbsideautocare,
clearlineautocare, silverlineautocare, frontlineautocare, steadfastautocare,
keystoneautocare, openroadautocare, ridgelineautocare.

**You still have to check the name on Sunbiz yourself.** Sunbiz sits behind a bot
wall that blocks automated lookups, so I could not do this part for you. Go to
`search.sunbiz.org` → Search Records → Entity Name, and type the name without the
"LLC". Florida rejects names that are **deceptively similar**, not only identical — so
search the distinctive word on its own (`Truepoint`), never the whole string. A
search for the full name returning nothing proves nothing.

If the name is refused after you have paid, you resubmit with a different name,
and the state does not generally refund filing fees. That is why you check
first, and why you go in with three.

Check your top three, in order, so you have a fallback ready while you are
sitting in the form.

Say the name out loud on a phone call before you commit. If the other person has
to ask "how do you spell that?", pick a different one.

### 2. Principal place of business address

A real street address, no PO box. This is 6160 Wiles Rd unless the answer to the
occupancy question changes it.

### 3. Registered agent

This can be you. It is free and there is no reason to pay a service $100/year.

Requirements: a **Florida street address**, no PO box, where somebody is
reachable during normal business hours. You type your name as the electronic
signature accepting the role.

**Know this before you type it:** the registered agent name and address become
**public record on Sunbiz**, searchable by anyone. That is unavoidable for any
Florida LLC — but it is worth knowing, because you are simultaneously hiding that
same address on your Google profile as a service-area business. The two are
separate systems and Google does not read Sunbiz, so this does not break the
Google strategy. It just means the address is not truly private.

### 4. Who is on it, and how

You and Rasul both go on as members. In the e-file form, the title to use for a
member who can act for the company is **AMBR** (Authorized Member) — use that for
both of you, not MGR, since you are running it yourselves rather than hiring an
outside manager.

Each of you needs full legal name and address.

Separately, agree the ownership split in writing — 50/50 or otherwise. It does not
go on the Articles, but the bank will ask, and it is far easier to agree now than
after the first good month.

## The form, field by field

`sunbiz.org` → E-Filing Services → **Florida Limited Liability Company**

1. **Effective date** — optional. Leave blank to start the day it is processed.
   You can backdate up to 5 business days or forward-date up to 90. If you are
   filing in late December, forward-date to January 1 so you do not owe an annual
   report for a year you barely existed in.
2. **LLC name** — exactly as decided, including the LLC suffix.
3. **Principal place of business address** — street address.
4. **Mailing address** — tick "same as principal" unless you have a reason.
5. **Registered agent name and Florida street address** — you.
6. **Registered agent signature** — type your name. That is the legal signature.
7. **Manager/Authorized Member** — add two entries, both **AMBR**, with names and
   addresses.
8. **FEI/EIN** — you do not have one yet. Select **"Applied For."** Do not delay
   the filing to get an EIN first; the EIN needs the LLC to exist.
9. **Correspondence email** — the address you actually read. Every state notice,
   including the annual report reminder, goes here.
10. **Signature of authorized person** — your typed name.
11. **Pay $125** by card.

Processing is typically a few business days. You get a confirmation letter with
the **document number** — save it, you need it for the EIN, the bank and the
annual report.

## The moment it is approved, in this order

Each one blocks the next. Same day if you can.

1. **EIN** — `irs.gov` → Apply for an EIN Online. Free, instant, takes ten
   minutes. **Anyone charging you for an EIN is running a scam.**
2. **Business bank account** — bring the filed Articles and the EIN letter. Every
   dollar of the business goes through it. Mixing it with your personal account is
   how you lose the liability protection you just paid $125 for.
3. **Florida sales tax, Form DR-1** — `floridarevenue.com`. Free. Before the first
   paid car. Broward is 7%, and the whole bill is taxable once chemicals, wax,
   coating or film transfer to the customer.
4. **Broward County business tax receipt** — `browardtax.org`.
5. **City of Coral Springs receipt** — (954) 344-5964. Home-based needs a utility
   bill or lease, a notarized affidavit, and police review.
6. **Insurance** — general liability plus **garage keepers**. Never touch a
   customer's car before this is bound.

## One thing you can now skip

**Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting is no longer required.**

FinCEN's rule of March 26 2025, since made permanent, redefines "reporting
company" to cover only entities formed under foreign law. All entities created in
the United States — yours included — are exempt from filing BOI.

This matters for two reasons. Sunbiz still displays a notice saying BOI reporting
is a federal requirement; that notice is **out of date**. And there is a large
scam industry charging $150–200 to "file your BOI before the deadline." You owe
nothing and you file nothing. Ignore those emails and letters.
